THE DG METHOD

App Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. Updated October 30, 2026 for version 1.1: nutrition, coaching tools, and your weekly schedule.

The DG Method app is a coaching app made by Daniel Goulson (DG Method) for his training clients. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you control it. The short version: we only keep what's needed to coach you, we never sell it, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign in details. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email. If you choose Hide My Email, we only get a private relay address from Apple.
  • An account ID. A random ID that links your workouts to your account.
  • Your training. The workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weights, and your red, yellow, or green effort ratings.
  • Your nutrition. The foods you log, how much you ate, their calories and macros, the daily targets your coach sets for you, and a list of foods you've logged before with your usual amount (My Foods).
  • Messages and coaching. Messages between you and your coach, the questions you ask Ask DG and its answers, call times your coach schedules, and your coach's weekly goals and notes.
  • Your body weight. The weigh ins you choose to log.
  • Your week. The Monday to Sunday schedule your coach sets, any days you move around, and the active rest days you mark as done.

Search and barcodes

Only the words you search or the barcode number are sent to the USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts databases, and barcode numbers to UPCitemdb to look up a product's name. Never your name or account. Everyday foods like bananas and eggs are built into the app and never leave your phone.

🏷️

FatSecret lookups

Barcodes can also be checked against fatsecret's food database. The app sends the barcode number to DG Method's own small server, hosted by Cloudflare, which asks fatsecret. The server checks that the request comes from the real app and a signed in client, but it doesn't pass your name or email to fatsecret.

🤝

Foods you add from a label

If a barcode isn't in any database, you can type it in from the label. That food's name, brand, serving size, nutrition and barcode are then shared with every DG Method client so it scans for them too. It's saved with a random account ID so your coach can fix mistakes, but your name is never shown to other clients.

📷

Meal photos

If you use the photo estimate, that photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI, through Google Firebase, to estimate what's on your plate. DG Method never stores your photos.

✨

Describe it with AI

If you type a description of a meal for an AI estimate, that text is sent to Gemini the same way. Nothing else about you is included.

🎯

Targets and logs

Your food logs and daily targets are saved to your account so your coach can see how you're eating and adjust your plan.

Google may keep and use photos and descriptions sent through its free Gemini service to improve its products. Please keep people and anything private out of meal photos. You can always search, scan, or type food in by hand instead.

NEW IN VERSION 1.1

Coaching tools

The Coach tab and your weekly schedule. Here's what each part does with your information.

💬

Messages with your coach

Messages you send your coach, and his replies, are saved to your account so you both see the same conversation on any phone.

✨

Ask DG

When you ask Ask DG a question, the question is sent to Google's Gemini AI through Google Firebase, along with the details it needs to give a useful answer: your first name, program, daily targets, what you've eaten today, recent lifts and weight trend. Your Ask DG chat stays private to you. Your coach only sees a question if you tap Send to coach.

⚖️

Weight tracking

Weigh ins you log are saved to your account and shown to you as a trend. Your coach can see them so he can adjust your plan.

📅

Your week and calls

Your schedule, any days you swap, and the active rest days you finish are saved to your account and visible to your coach. Call reminders are set on your phone itself. The app only adds a call to your calendar when you tap Add to Calendar, and it can't read your calendar.

The same Google note applies to Ask DG questions. Please don't put anything in Ask DG you wouldn't want stored, like medical records, and send anything personal or medical to your coach instead.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health records, or browsing activity, and we only use your camera when you choose to scan a barcode or take a meal photo. The app has no ads and no ad tracking, and it does not use analytics to follow you across apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To show you your program and suggest your next weights.
  • To track your eating against your daily targets, and make foods you've had before quick to log again.
  • To let you message your coach, see your weekly plan and calls, and track your weight.
  • To answer everyday training and nutrition questions through Ask DG.
  • To let your coach, Daniel Goulson, see your workouts, food, weight, schedule and messages so he can coach you.
  • To keep your training saved if you change phones.

Who can see it

Only you and your coach. Your information is stored with Google Firebase, a service that hosts the app's accounts and database in the United States. Google processes it only to run the app (meal photos, food descriptions and Ask DG questions are the exceptions, explained above). We never sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, workouts, food logs, messages, weigh ins and schedule for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap the account icon at the top of your program, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, every workout and food you've logged, your saved foods, messages, weigh ins, weekly notes and schedule, and removes DG Method's access to your Apple ID. Foods you added from a label stay in the shared list for other clients, since they don't include your name. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
