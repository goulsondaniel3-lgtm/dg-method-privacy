THE DG METHOD

App Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

The DG Method app is a coaching app made by Daniel Goulson (DG Method) for his training clients. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you control it. The short version: we only keep what's needed to coach you, we never sell it, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign in details. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email. If you choose Hide My Email, we only get a private relay address from Apple.
  • An account ID. A random ID that links your workouts to your account.
  • Your training. The workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weights, and your red, yellow, or green effort ratings.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health records, or browsing activity. The app has no ads and no ad tracking, and it does not use analytics to follow you across apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To show you your program and suggest your next weights.
  • To let your coach, Daniel Goulson, see your workouts so he can coach you.
  • To keep your training saved if you change phones.

Who can see it

Only you and your coach. Your information is stored with Google Firebase, a service that hosts the app's accounts and database in the United States. Google processes it only to run the app. We never sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and workouts for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap the account icon at the top of your program, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and every workout you've logged, and removes DG Method's access to your Apple ID. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
